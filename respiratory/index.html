About This Data

  • ED visit rates are aggregated weekly from emergency departments and urgent care facilities participating in the national syndromic surveillance program.
  • Wastewater surveillance reflects viral genetic material measured in sewage samples, an early indicator of community transmission that is independent of testing or healthcare-seeking behavior. Samples are collected by the City of Lawrence and submitted for analysis to WastewaterSCAN, a nationwide wastewater monitoring program based at Standord University in partnership with Emory University.
  • During the flu season (October through May) LDCPH refreshes this data on a weekly basis; see the "Data updated" badge above for the most recent refresh date.
  • This dashboard is for general public health situational awareness and should not be used for individual medical decision-making.
  • This content was developed with the aid of one or more Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools, primarily to generate HTML, CSS and Javascript code. Human oversight was applied to review and verify the output.
